The spending map

Use each part of Zurich differently.

Kreis 3 & 4

Everyday Zurich

Use for groceries, casual meals, cafes and the Helvetiaplatz market. Spend on a local dinner or a specific event; skip aimless bar-hopping and daily convenience shops.

Zurich West

Workday value

Use for a Lidl run, free contemporary art and industrial riverside walks. Spend on a concert or a planned dinner; not premium lunch because it is nearby.

Old Town

Walk first, then choose

Use for churches, galleries and a museum. Pay for one historic meal or drink if you want it; skip generic Swiss menus and souvenir browsing.

Lake & Enge

Free luxury

Use for a picnic, swim, walk or sunset. Pay for a lakeside meal only when the view is the point; bring water and avoid tourist add-ons.
